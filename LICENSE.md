# Licences

**Data:** each layer keeps the licence of its source, recorded per layer in `layers.json`,
the STAC items (`stac/`) and `LAYERS.md`. Where no licence is recorded yet, the source agency
is named and the data is published for research and teaching while terms are confirmed.
